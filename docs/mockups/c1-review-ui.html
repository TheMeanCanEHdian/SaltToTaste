Nutrition review C1: zeros, order, grams on confirm, per batch

Four changes to the shipped review surfaces (26e57e2): (A) a zero-gram row below the gate stops naming a wrong food; (B) the queue opens on the groups whose decision finishes the most recipes; (C) a line with no grams is confirmed with its amount in the same step; (D) a MAKES-yield recipe says its serving is the batch. A · A zero is a zero, not a food

An engine 0 g row (gram_source unmeasured or discarded, grams = 0) is already counted by the bucket rule and adds nothing, so it never reaches the queue. It only shows up in the per-recipe review sheet's "Counted — looks good" list, which everyone can read (review_sheet.dart:12-14). Below 50% the food named there is often a wrong guess. A cook who reads "Gel food dye → Fast foods, coleslaw" stops trusting every other line. Decision: below 0.5 the row says "Counts as zero (no amount)", or "(discarded in cooking)" for the one discarded row, a new short wording. It is not gramSourceLabel's text: for unmeasured that returns "no amount on the line" (its bare "no amount" is only the fallback for a null or unknown source); "discarded in cooking" does match (match_fix_panel.dart:43-52). The food name is hidden and the badge is a grey counts as zero in place of the green "counted". The guess is not deleted. Admins can open it from a collapsed Engine guess — not used disclosure, and it appears in the fix sheet as a struck-through candidate that is not selected. Members never see it. This touches 28 rows: 27 unmeasured, 1 discarded.

C · Grams belong to the confirm

Under the new rule, a confirm with no grams keeps the line in No grams and the recipe partial. A "Confirm" that leaves the label unchanged is a second trip waiting to happen, so here Confirm carries its amount. When the food is right and only the amount is missing, the amount block comes first. It shows what the line says, the record's real USDA portions, and why the engine could not convert. The primary button follows the number ("Confirm · 452 g"). Until a number is in, it reads "Confirm with amount" and stays disabled. When the line's unit names one of the record's portions, that portion is a tap-to-fill chip (4 × stick = 452 g), so the whole gesture is two taps. Portions that don't match the line's unit are listed for reference only and can't be tapped. Tapping "5 slices = 11 g" into a field for "1 piece" would write the wrong quantity.

Check lines below 0.5 with no grams (166 in the snapshot)

An engine pick below 0.5 is stored without fetching the food's detail, and {confirmed: true} fetches it once and converts the line's own amount (API.md, PUT matches). So on these lines the amount field is optional. With it empty, the button reads "Confirm" and the server converts. If that still leaves no grams, the line lands in No grams and the pane stays on it with the field focused, which is one extra step only when USDA can't convert. The group row's warning "— Confirm as-is is unavailable" (nutrition_review_queue.dart:630) becomes "— confirm asks for the amount". When the food is wrong as well, the shipped "Save match & amount" already takes both in one save.

New wire data C needs

The portion list and kcal per 100 g come from fdc_food_cache, cache-only, as a new portions field on the matches body. Reading the matches never spends FDC budget (API.md). When the detail was never fetched, the list is absent and the block says "USDA portions load after the first confirm". This new field goes into API.md in the same commit (open question 1).

D · Per batch, said out loud (the rule is being built now; the outlined tag is a proposal)

When a MAKES-style yield sets a serving basis of 1, the label reads "Per serving · serves 1" over 2,336 kcal, which looks like an error. As being built (working tree, not shipped, so it may still change): basisKindOf marks any recipe with a basis of 2 or less and no serves as per batch, whatever its yield text, and the header prints it as plain text, "Per serving · serves 1 · per batch". That is 57 recipes in the snapshot: 28 at basis 1 and 29 at basis 2, all MAKES yields except Latin Flan, which has no servings text. New proposal on top of the build: a small outlined black per batch tag beside the header (nutrition_label.dart:389), inside the label so it reads as part of the panel and not as app chrome, with one line under it naming the yield it came from (Latin Flan has none to name, so it would get the tag alone). The mock draws the basis-1 case. Proposed change, not yet agreed (open question 3): with basis 2 (Ciabatta "MAKES 2 LOAVES", Sourdough Starter "MAKES ABOUT 2 CUPS") a serving is half the batch, and "per batch" would mislabel it (API.md:613-618). Yields that are a single portion ("MAKES 1 OMELET", "MAKES 1 COCKTAIL", "MAKES 1 TO 16 EGGS") would be left out, so 24 of the 27 basis-1 MAKES recipes would get the tag.
