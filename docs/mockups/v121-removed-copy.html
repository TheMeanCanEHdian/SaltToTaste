Three wordings for ingredients the steps take away

A copy delta to the approved nutrition mockups. Each string is shown on a real row from the library with real figures. No calorie figure on this page changes: these are the words the row shows, not the amounts. Rows use the approved layout; only the parts marked new are new.

1 · A paste or marinade scraped off the food: stays counted, with a note

The owner's ruling of 2026-11-19. The steps scrape the mixture off but a residue stays on the food and is eaten, so the row keeps its full amount and says it is approximate. The wording mirrors the note already shipped for a liquid marinade.

2 · Salt read from the steps, when part of it goes down the drain

Built in Matcher v121. A line such as “Table salt” prints no amount, so the label reads the amounts the steps print. Salt that goes into pasta water, a soak or a brine is left out, and the row says how much was left out. The first half of the line is shipped wording; the highlighted half is new. The batch was built with the wording below; the owner approved it as built.

3 · A meat the recipe simmers and then discards: the review card's reason

Held rows show a reason on their review card. Rows like this one borrow the reason written for liquids, which does not describe a chicken wing. The proposal is a reason and a button label of their own. Nothing about how the row is held or counted changes.
