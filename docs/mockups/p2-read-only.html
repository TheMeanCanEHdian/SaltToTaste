A Piece of Cake

Rich Chocolate Bundt Cake

dessert
Serves🍽12
Prep⏱20m
Bake🔥50m

A Bundt cake is the pinnacle of cake-baking simplicity. We intensified the chocolate flavor with both bittersweet chocolate and natural cocoa, blooming them in boiling water; sour cream and brown sugar add moisture.

Ingredients
  • 12 tablespoons (1½ sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • ¾ cup natural cocoa powder, plus 1 tablespoon for the pan
  • 6 ounces bittersweet chocolate, chopped coarse
  • 1 teaspoon instant espresso powder (optional)
  • ¾ cup boiling water
  • 1 cup sour cream, at room temperature
  • 1¾ cups (8¾ ounces) unbleached all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups packed (14 ounces) light brown sugar
  • 5 large eggs, at room temperature
Directions
1Stir together the 1 tablespoon melted butter and 1 tablespoon cocoa into a paste; brush all interior surfaces of a 12-cup Bundt pan. Heat the oven to 350 degrees.
2Combine the remaining cocoa, chocolate, and espresso powder. Pour the boiling water over and whisk until smooth; cool, then whisk in the sour cream.
3Beat the butter, brown sugar, and vanilla until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, then alternate the flour and chocolate mixtures until just combined.
4Transfer to the pan and bake until a toothpick comes out with a few crumbs, 45 to 50 minutes. Cool 10 minutes, invert, and dust with confectioners' sugar.
