Nutrition review v40: composite rows

v40 adds one new row type, the composite row: one ingredient line whose nutrients are not a single USDA record. It has two uses. (1) Sub-recipe routing, phase 1 (plan.md Q7): a line such as "1 recipe double-crust pie dough" counts the child recipe's stored totals × a share, with no FDC id and gram_source recipe. (2) Rendered bacon (plan.md Q5 a): one line counts as cooked bacon plus the fat kept in the pan. Every figure here comes from plan.md or subrecipe.md. Raw lines, titles and servings come from the ATK corpus files named in data-src. A figure neither file gives is drawn as a dashed slot, never guessed.

  • No food, no FDC chip. The row names the child recipe, and its data-type chip reads Recipe in the brand tint. The engine writes no USDA description, because there is no record.
  • The share always shows, even at 1. At ⅔ the row reads "⅔ of Quick Tomato Sauce for Pizza (1 cup of about 1½ cups)" (subrecipe.md §C3).

  • ‹g› is the dough's stored total_grams × 1. Neither source file prints it, so the build fills it in.
2 · The fix sheet for a composite row (Change…)

The panel looks like the food fix panel, but it chooses a recipe, not a food. Candidates come in the resolution order (plan.md Q7): the parent's own section, then library recipes the note names or whose title matches exactly, then another recipe's section, labelled with the recipe that holds it. A fuzzy title is never chosen automatically, but a person may pick one. There is no "Search USDA" row and no food candidate.

How the row reads, and what it offers
  • One line, one row, one badge. The two records are listed under the line, each with its own grams and FDC chip. The grams of the two parts add up to what the row counts (153 g), not to the 284 g on the line. The basis line says why.
  • No hold, no queue. The row is counted and flagged, like any approximate row. It reaches the review queue only through the queue's existing rules.
  • Change opens the usual food fix panel. Picking a single food replaces both parts with that food at the line's own grams. This is how a person undoes the rule on a line. Confirm keeps both parts. Skip excludes the line, as today.
  • The wider lines (oven-fried bacon, quiche Lorraine, carbonara; plan.md Q5 c) are left to a person. v40 draws no button for them. See open question 3.
  • The brief quoted the line as "10 slices bacon, chopped". The corpus line (0040, line 5) is the one shown here.

Open questions for the owner
  1. The choose_recipe bucket. Should it get its own chip, or sit inside No match? Recommend its own chip. The fix is a recipe, not a USDA food, and No match's panel offers a food search.
  2. Confirm on a defaulted dough. Does a person's Confirm of the note's first dough clear the approximation flag? Recommend yes. A person chose it, just as a confirmed food row loses its confidence warning.
  3. The wider bacon lines (plan.md Q5 c: oven-fried bacon, quiche Lorraine, carbonara). Should a person get a "count as rendered" action with a kept-fat field? Recommend not in v40. Typed grams already let a person correct those lines, and the action can follow if the queue shows a need.
  4. Section candidates (phase 2, no totals yet). Listed but not pickable, or hidden until v41? Recommend listed and not pickable, so a person sees why a "recipes follow" line is held.
  5. The label's "Includes N recipe" line is shown to members too. Recommend yes. A cook should see that the pie's calories include a dough the note only suggests.

Not drawn: the child recipe's own review sheet (it is the shipped sheet), the stale banner (shipped), and the 0 g sub-recipe row before v40 (shipped).
