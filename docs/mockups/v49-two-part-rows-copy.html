Two-part rows: the role copy delta (after matcher v49)

A delta to the approved composite-row mockup (v41-composite-rows.html §4, the rendered-bacon row: "matched to two records, cooked and drained:" with the second part suffixed "· kept in the pan"). Matcher v49 writes two more kinds of two-part row and sends each part's role on the wire: zest | juice (a zest over a tablespoon plus juice) and drained | undrained (a can drained and a can kept with its liquid). The shipped app shows them with the bacon's words removed ("matched to two records:", no suffixes) as an interim guard. This delta gives each role pair its own header and suffixes, in the bacon row's shape. The bacon row is unchanged. Every figure below is the row the v49 replay writes.

Eleven lines carry this pair after v49 (the orange beef, the lemon chicken, key lime pie, the margaritas, the lemon cakes and puddings, the swordfish skewers, the fava beans). A line whose zest is strained out later (lemon-tart line 4) is a one-record juice row and keeps the shipped basis "juice only (the zest is strained out)" — no two-part copy. The lime lines' peel reads the lemon-peel record and carries the engine's flag "approximation (counted as Lemon peel, raw)" on the flag line as today.
