2 · Match review sheet — per-ingredient provenance (opens from the badge)

One row per ingredient line: the raw text, the matched FDC food (with dataType), the resolved grams with how they were derived, and a confidence pill. Admin actions per row: Confirm ✓ (locks the auto-match), Change… (candidate picker — open below on the espresso row), Set grams (the grams box is directly editable), Skip. Confirmed/overridden rows persist across recomputes. Members see this sheet read-only, without the action buttons.

Serving basis

Per-serving numbers divide the recipe total by the lower bound of the parsed servings — “SERVES 8 TO 10” computes per 8 (the conservative estimate). The basis is editable per recipe in its match review sheet; recipes whose servings don't parse (e.g. “MAKES 1 QUART”) get totals only, no per-serving label, and are excluded from calories: search.

5 · Search tie-in — calories: goes live

The DSL's calories: scope (parsed since P0, matching nothing until now) compiles to a predicate on computed per-serving calories. Calorie-filtered results order ascending by default (lowest first) and exclude recipes without computed nutrition — the result count reflects only computed matches, and the eyebrow says so.
